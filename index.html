✦ OpenCreative
OPEN SOURCE • LOCAL FIRST
Your creative workspace

Make something
remarkable.

A free, browser-based creative studio. Start with practical tools for images, vectors, video, audio and PDFs. Your files stay on your device unless you export or share them.

No account required
Early access: This is an independent open-source project, not an Adobe product or a complete replacement for every professional Adobe feature. The tools below are real local-first starting points; more advanced workflows are planned.
🖼️

Pixel Studio

Draw, erase, add text and shapes, open images and export PNG artwork.

✒️

Vector Studio

Create scalable SVG compositions with editable shapes and text.

🎬

Video Studio

Preview a local clip, set an in/out range and export a trimmed recording where supported.

🎙️

Audio Studio

Record microphone audio locally and download it as an audio file.

📄

PDF Desk

Open and preview a local PDF using your browser's built-in PDF viewer.

🗂️

Project Vault

Save and reopen your vector project as a portable JSON file.

Vector Studio

Drag on the artboard to add a shape. Select Text to place a text label. SVG remains resolution-independent.

Video Studio
