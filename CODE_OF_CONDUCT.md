# Code of Conduct

OpenCreative is a community for people with different backgrounds, skills, and experience levels. We want participation to be welcoming, constructive, and safe.

## Our expectations

- Be kind, patient, and respectful.
- Welcome beginners and explain technical concepts without ridicule.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give feedback that is specific, constructive, and relevant.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult, discriminate against, or intimidate others.
- Do not share private information, spam, or content unrelated to the project.
- Respect intellectual property, licenses, and attribution requirements.

## Scope

This applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community interactions representing OpenCreative.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project owner through the GitHub profile or repository contact options. Include links and relevant context where possible. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or close contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Our goal

Make OpenCreative a place where people can learn, build, collaborate, and disagree respectfully.
